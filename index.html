FARD (Obligatory Pillar) Step 1: Departure & Miqat Intention الإحرام والميقات وتجريد النية
Al-Ihram, Al-Miqat wa Tajreed an-Niyyah
Ritual Foundation
Essential Du'a & Supplication
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً. اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أُرِيدُ الْعُمْرَةَ فَيَسِّرْهَا لِي وَتَقَبَّلْهَا مِنِّي. لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ.
Labbayka Allahumma 'Umrah. Allahumma inni uridu al-'umrata fa-yassirha li wa taqabbalha minni. Labbayk Allahumma Labbayk, Labbayka Laa Shareeka Laka Labbayk, Innal-Hamda Wan-Ni'mata Laka Wal-Mulk, Laa Shareeka Lak.
"Here I am, O Allah, answering Your call to perform Umrah. O Allah, I intend to perform Umrah, so make it easy for me and accept it from me. Here I am at Your service, O Allah, here I am. Here I am at Your service, You have no partner, here I am. Truly all praise, grace, and sovereignty belong to You. You have no partner."
Historical & Spiritual Significance
The Miqats are designated geographic boundaries established by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ where pilgrims must not pass without donning the Ihram and making the sacred intention. The primary Miqats include Dhul Hulaifah (Abyar Ali) for pilgrims coming from Madinah, Al-Juhfah (near Rabigh) for travelers from the Levant, Egypt, and Europe, Qarn al-Manazil (Al-Sail al-Kabir) for those coming from Riyadh, Najd, Taif, and the East/Air routes, Yalamlam for Yemen and the southern sea/air routes, and Dhat Irq for travelers from Iraq. Ihram signifies total surrender, humility, and equality before the Creator, shedding worldly distinctions of wealth and status.
Visiting Hours & Timing
Anytime before passing the designated Miqat boundary. For flights landing in Jeddah or Madinah, Ihram preparation must take place before boarding or at least 45 minutes prior to landing when the pilot announces the boundary crossing.
Mode of Transport & Logistics
Commercial flight announcements notify passengers when approaching the Miqat. If traveling by road from Madinah, the air-conditioned Haramain High-Speed Train provides announcement chimes or highway coaches stop at Abyar Ali Mosque.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Trim nails, remove unwanted hair, perform Ghusl (ritual bath), apply perfume to the body (not the Ihram garments) before making intention. Men wear two unstitched white seamless cloths (Izar and Rida) with footwear leaving the ankles and bridge of feet bare. Women wear modest loose clothing with face and hands uncovered. Once Niyyah is made, all 9 prohibitions (Mahzurat) take immediate effect.

LOGISTICAL TRANSITION Step 2: Arrival Jeddah & Transit to Makkah الوصول إلى جدة والانتقال إلى مكة المكرمة
Al-Wusul ila Jiddah wal-Intiqal ila Makkah
Travel & Transit
Essential Du'a & Supplication
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الصَّاحِبُ فِي السَّفَرِ، وَالْخَلِيفَةُ فِي الأَهْلِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ وَعْثَاءِ السَّفَرِ وَكَآبَةِ الْمَنْظَرِ وَسُوءِ الْمُنْقَلَبِ.
Allahumma Antas-Sahibu fis-safar, wal-Khalifatu fil-ahl, Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min wa'tha'is-safari wa ka'abatil-manzari wa su'il-munqalab.
"O Allah, You are the Companion in travel and the Protector of our family left behind. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the hardships of travel, distressing sights, and an unfortunate return."
Historical & Spiritual Significance
Jeddah has stood as the maritime and aerial gateway to the Two Holy Sanctuaries for over fourteen centuries since the era of Caliph Uthman ibn Affan RA. Today, King Abdulaziz International Airport (KAIA) welcomes millions of pilgrims from across the globe into its modern Terminal 1 and North Terminal, facilitating seamless movement towards the ancient sanctuary of Bakkah.
Visiting Hours & Timing
24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Peak arrival rushes occur in the late afternoon and after evening international long-haul flights.
Mode of Transport & Logistics
Haramain High-Speed Railway: Station is integrated directly inside Terminal 1. Sleek 300 km/h electric trains whisk pilgrims to Makkah Rusaifah Station in just 45–55 minutes. Alternatively: Pre-booked Careem/Uber, authorized airport limousines, or SAPTCO express luxury coaches running round the clock.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Maintain continuous Talbiyah chanting in the vehicle. Have your passport, Saudi visa barcode, and Nusuk app ready on your phone. Stay hydrated with water provided at the terminals. Avoid arguments or agitation during luggage collection and customs clearance, as patience is an essential spiritual discipline of Ihram.

SUNNAH ETIQUETTE Step 3: Hotel Check-in & Entering Al-Masjid al-Haram دخول مكة المكرمة والاستعداد للبيت الحرام
Dukhul Makkah wal-Isti'dad lil-Bayt al-Haram
Spiritual Entry
Essential Du'a & Supplication
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ. اللَّهُمَّ زِدْ هَذَا الْبَيْتَ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَمَهَابَةً، وَزِدْ مَنْ شَرَّفَهُ وَعَظَّمَهُ مِمَّنْ حَجَّهُ أَوِ اعْتَمَرَهُ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَبِرًّا.
Bismillahi was-salatu was-salamu 'ala Rasulillah. Allahummaftah li abwaba rahmatik. Allahumma zid hadhal-bayta tashreefan wa ta'dheeman wa takreeman wa mahabah, wa zid man sharrafahu wa 'adh-dhamahu mimman hajjahu awi'tamarahu tashreefan wa takreeman wa ta'dheeman wa birra.
"In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings be upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the gates of Your mercy. O Allah, increase this House in honor, reverence, nobility, and awe, and increase whoever honors and reveres it through Hajj or Umrah in honor, nobility, greatness, and righteousness."
Historical & Spiritual Significance
Entering Makkah with humility, veneration, and peaceful devotion follows the Sunnah of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, who entered on the day of Fath Makkah with his head bowed so low in gratitude to Allah that his beard almost touched his saddle. Al-Masjid al-Haram is the most sacred sanctuary on earth, wherein a single prayer carries the multiplication of 100,000 prayers.
Visiting Hours & Timing
Open continuously 24/7. Entering the Mataf for Umrah is easiest during off-peak windows (between 10:00 PM and 3:00 AM, or between 9:00 AM and 11:30 AM outside Friday rush).
Mode of Transport & Logistics
Walk from Central Haram hotels (Clock Tower / Jabal Omar / Ajyad / Shamiyah). For outlying hotels in Aziziyah or Kudai, utilize dedicated hotel shuttles or public SAPTCO drop-offs at Kudai or Jarwal transport stations.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Drop heavy luggage safely at your hotel room, take a refreshing wudu, pack your footwear into a lightweight drawstring shoe bag (keep it with you at all times, never leave shoes outside gates where they can be lost), keep your mobile charged with Nusuk permit displayed. Enter the Haram with your right foot first, reciting the entrance supplication. The moment your eyes first gaze upon the Holy Kaaba, make earnest du'a, as supplications at the first sight of the House are accepted.

FARD (Obligatory Pillar) Step 4: Tawaf al-Umrah (7 Circuits of the Kaaba) طواف العمرة سبعة أشواط حول الكعبة المشرفة
Tawaf al-'Umrah Sab'atu Ashwat
Sacred Circumambulation
Essential Du'a & Supplication
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ. رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ. سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، وَلَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ.
Bismillahi Allahu Akbar. (Between Rukn Yamani and Hajar Aswad): Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar. Subhanallahi wal-hamdulillahi wa la ilaha illallahu wallahu akbar, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil-'Aliyyil-'Adheem.
"In the name of Allah, Allah is the Greatest. Our Lord! Grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the torment of the Fire. Glory be to Allah, all praise is due to Allah, there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, Allah is the Greatest, and there is no power nor strength except with Allah, the Most High, the Supreme."
Historical & Spiritual Significance
Tawaf represents the perpetual cosmic dance of all creation glorifying the One True Lord, mirroring the angelic circumambulation of Al-Bayt al-Ma'mur in the highest heavens. Initiated by Prophet Ibrahim AS and revived in pristine monotheism by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the seven circuits around the ancient cubic House symbolize the surrender of the heart and soul to divine unity.
Visiting Hours & Timing
Available 24 hours. The ground-floor Mataf is reserved specifically for pilgrims wearing the white Ihram. First floor and roof accommodate strollers, wheelchairs, and electric carts.
Mode of Transport & Logistics
Accessible directly via Bab King Abdulaziz (Gate 1), Bab King Fahd (Gate 79), or Bab al-Salam. Electric single/double carts for elderly and disabled pilgrims are available on the upper mezzanine/roof level via ticket booths at Ajyad and Shubaika ramps.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
For men: Uncover the right shoulder (Idtiba') for all 7 circuits. Start each circuit aligned with the Black Stone (green neon indicator on the Haram wall), raise your right hand towards Hajar al-Aswad, and say 'Bismillahi Allahu Akbar'. During circuits 1, 2, and 3, men perform Ramal (brisk, purposeful stride with puffed chest) if crowding permits. Circuit 4 to 7 are walked normally. Touch the Yemeni Corner with right hand if possible without kissing or jostling. Do not enter the Hateem (semi-circle) during Tawaf, as it is inside the Kaaba; passing through it invalidates that lap. After completing 7 laps, cover the right shoulder completely. Pray 2 Rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim (or anywhere in the Haram if crowded), reciting Surah Al-Kafirun in the 1st rak'ah and Surah Al-Ikhlas in the 2nd.

SUNNAH MUSTAHABBAH Step 5: Drinking Zamzam Water & Supplication الشرب من ماء زمزم المبارك والتضلع
Ash-Shurb min Ma' Zamzam wat-Tadallu'
Sacred Hydration
Essential Du'a & Supplication
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا وَاسِعًا، وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ وَسَقَمٍ.
Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an, wa rizqan wasi'an, wa shifa'an min kulli da'in wa saqam.
"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, abundant and wholesome provision, and a cure for every disease and ailment."
Historical & Spiritual Significance
The miraculous spring of Zamzam gushed forth under the kicking feet of infant Prophet Ismail AS when his mother, Sayyidah Hajar, ran desperately between the arid hills of Safa and Marwa. For thousands of years, this water has quenched the thirst of billions of pilgrims. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ declared: 'The water of Zamzam is for whatever purpose it is drunk for' (Ibn Majah), calling it nutritious food and a healing remedy for ailments.
Visiting Hours & Timing
Dispensers, barrels, and chilled/room-temperature thermoses are situated continuously along every meter of the Mataf perimeter, the underground basement, and the Sa'i galleries 24 hours daily.
Mode of Transport & Logistics
Located directly on the pathway transitioning from the Tawaf Mataf to the Masa'a (Safa and Marwa concourse).
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Drink with the right hand while facing the Qiblah. It is recommended to drink to total satisfaction (Tadallu' - filling the stomach). Recite Bismillah before drinking, make your personal heartfelt du'a for spiritual illumination, healing, and knowledge, and gently pour a small splash over your head and face in accordance with the Prophetic practice. Respect cleanliness: do not wash feet in Zamzam thermoses and dispose of single-use cups into designated recycling bins.

FARD (Obligatory Pillar) Step 6: Sa'i between Safa & Marwa (7 Courses) السعي بين الصفا والمروة سبعة أشواط
As-Sa'i baynas-Safa wal-Marwah Sab'atu Ashwat
Devotional Striving
Essential Du'a & Supplication
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ فَمَنْ حَجَّ الْبَيْتَ أَوِ اعْتَمَرَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْهِ أَنْ يَطَّوَّفَ بِهِمَا وَمَنْ تَطَوَّعَ خَيْرًا فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ شَاكِرٌ عَلِيمٌ. نَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللَّهُ بِهِ. اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ.
Innas-Safa wal-Marwata min sha'a'irillah, faman hajjal-bayta awi'tamara fala junaha 'alayhi an yattawwafa bihima, wa man tatawwa'a khayran fa-innallaha Shakirun 'Aleem. Nabda'u bima bada'allahu bih. Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar, wa lillahil-hamd. La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la shareeka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu yuhyi wa yumeetu wa Huwa 'ala kulli shay'in Qadeer. La ilaha illallahu wahdah, anjaza wa'dah, wa nasara 'abdah, wa hazamal-ahzaba wahdah.
"Indeed, Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of Allah... We begin with what Allah began with. Allah is the Greatest (3x), and to Allah belongs all praise. There is no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone, without partner. To Him belongs all sovereignty and praise; He gives life and causes death, and He is over all things omnipotent. There is no deity except Allah alone; He fulfilled His promise, granted victory to His servant, and defeated the allied confederates alone."
Historical & Spiritual Significance
Sa'i commemorates the supreme maternal faith and endurance of Sayyidah Hajar, wife of Prophet Ibrahim AS, who ran seven times between the rocky outcrops of Mount Safa and Mount Marwa seeking water or a caravan for her baby Ismail in the barren, uncultivated desert valley of Makkah. Allah immortalized her perseverance as an eternal rite of Hajj and Umrah in the Holy Qur'an: 'Indeed, Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of Allah' (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:158).
Visiting Hours & Timing
Available 24/7 across multiple air-conditioned levels: Ground floor, 1st floor, 2nd floor, and roof. High-efficiency mechanical mist fans and air scrubbers ensure total climate control.
Mode of Transport & Logistics
Direct covered walkways lead from the Mataf ground floor to Mount Safa. The Masa'a provides dedicated lanes in the center for electric scooters, manual wheelchairs, and family carts.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Sa'i consists of exactly 7 laps: Lap 1 begins on Mount Safa and ends on Mount Marwa. Lap 2 is from Marwa to Safa. The 7th lap concludes at Mount Marwa. On Mount Safa, climb until Kaaba direction is clear, face Qiblah, raise hands, make Takbir and Tahleel three times, and make long personal du'a. For men only: Jog at an easy, spirited pace between the illuminated green overhead strip markers (Batn al-Wadi, commemorating where Hajar hastened in the depression). Women walk at normal pace throughout. Wudu is Sunnah and highly recommended during Sa'i, though if broken, Sa'i remains valid.

WAJIB (Compulsory Obligation) Step 7: Halq or Taqsir (Release from Ihram / Tahallul) الحلق أو التقصير والتحلل من الإحرام
Al-Halq aw at-Taqsir wat-Tahallul min al-Ihram
Sacred Conclusion
Essential Du'a & Supplication
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي قَضَى عَنَّا نُسُكَنَا. اللَّهُمَّ هَذِهِ نَاصِيَتِي بِيَدِكَ فَاجْعَلْ لِي بِكُلِّ شَعْرَةٍ نُورًا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ، وَاغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي وَتَقَبَّلْ مِنِّي عُمْرَتِي.
Al-Hamdulillahi alladhi qada 'anna nusukana. Allahumma hadhihi nasiyati biyadika faj'al li bikulli sha'ratin nooran yawmal-qiyamah, waghfir li dhanbi wa taqabbal minni 'umrati.
"All praise belongs to Allah Who enabled us to complete our sacred rites. O Allah, this is my forelock in Your Hand; make for me with every single hair a radiant light on the Day of Resurrection, forgive my transgressions, and accept my Umrah."
Historical & Spiritual Significance
Shaving or clipping the hair serves as the final physical submission of the pilgrim, sacrificing an emblem of personal vanity and beauty for the pleasure of Allah. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ invoked the special mercy and forgiveness of Allah three times for those who shave their entire head (Muhalliqin), and once for those who trim (Muqassirin): 'O Allah! Forgive those who shave their heads.' The Sahabah asked: 'And those who trim, O Messenger of Allah?' He repeated his prayer for the shavers twice more before adding: 'And those who trim' (Sahih al-Bukhari).
Visiting Hours & Timing
Open 24 hours. Numerous certified, hygienic barber shops operate directly at the exits of Mount Marwa, beneath the Makkah Clock Royal Tower concourse, and throughout Souq al-Khalil.
Mode of Transport & Logistics
Exit Marwa gates towards the outer courtyards. Descend escalators into the commercial basements of Clock Tower (Abraj Al-Bait) or Jabal Omar commercial galleries.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
For Men: Halq (complete head shave with clean, single-use disposable razor) carries the highest reward. Alternatively, Taqsir (trimming hair equally by cutting at least 1–2 cm across the entire head) is permissible. Do not merely snip a few stray hairs. For Women: Halq is prohibited; women gather their hair into a braid or ponytail and cut the length of a single fingertip (approximately 1 inch / 2.5 cm) from the ends. Once hair is shaved or trimmed, all Ihram restrictions are immediately lifted (Tahallul al-Kamil), and your Umrah is officially completed. Congratulations: Mabruk 'Umratukum!

Supreme Sanctuary The Holy Kaaba (Al-Ka'bah al-Musharrafah) الكعبة المشرفة - بيت الله العتيق
Al-Ka'bah al-Musharrafah - Baytullah al-'Ateeq
Core Haram
Historical Background & Significance
The epicenter of Islamic worship and the earthly Qiblah towards which over 1.8 billion Muslims turn five times daily. First raised by Prophet Adam AS and reconstructed upon pristine foundations by Prophet Ibrahim AS and his son Ismail AS under divine revelation. Wrapped in the hand-embroidered black silk Kiswa adorned with pure gold and silver calligraphic Quranic threads, the Kaaba stands as an enduring monument to absolute Tawhid.
Visiting Hours & Best Timings
Accessible 24 hours daily via the Mataf for pilgrims performing Tawaf. Friday midday congregational prayers experience immense crowd influx.
Mode of Transport & Access
Located in the heart of Al-Masjid al-Haram. Accessible through Gate 1 (King Abdulaziz Gate), Gate 79 (King Fahd Gate), and Bab al-Salam.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Touch the Yemeni Corner (Rukn al-Yamani) respectfully without pushing if within reach. Gaze upon the Kaaba with reverence and supplication; scholars emphasize that du'a upon sighting the Kaaba is specially granted. Follow the counter-clockwise flow of the crowd strictly.

Interior of Kaaba Al-Hateem (Hijr Ismail) الحطيم - حجر إسماعيل عليه السلام
Al-Hateem - Hijr Isma'il
Core Haram
Historical Background & Significance
The semi-circular white marble enclosure adjacent to the northwestern wall of the Kaaba. When the Quraysh rebuilt the Kaaba during the Prophet's ﷺ youth, pure funds ran short, forcing them to leave approximately three meters of the original Ibrahimi foundation outside the walled cube. Aisha RA asked the Prophet ﷺ if she could enter the House to pray; he gently took her hand into the Hijr and said: 'Pray in the Hijr if you wish to enter the House, for it is part of the House' (Sunan an-Nasa'i).
Visiting Hours & Best Timings
Opened periodically by Haram security guards outside mandatory prayer times and peak Tawaf rushes. Special queuing lines operate for men and women during designated windows.
Mode of Transport & Access
Situated directly on the ground floor Mataf courtyard along the northern side of the Kaaba under the Golden Water Spout (Mizab ar-Rahmah).
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Praying two rak'ahs inside the Hateem is identical to praying inside the physical walls of the Kaaba itself. Never walk inside the Hateem while executing your 7 Tawaf rounds, as that circumambulates inside the House and nullifies the circuit.

Heavenly Relic Al-Hajar al-Aswad (The Black Stone) الحجر الأسود - ياقوتة من الجنة
Al-Hajar al-Aswad - Yaqutatan min al-Jannah
Core Haram
Historical Background & Significance
Set into the eastern corner of the Kaaba, this heavenly stone was brought down by Archangel Jibril AS to Prophet Ibrahim AS during the building of the sanctuary. The Prophet ﷺ said: 'The Black Stone descended from Paradise whiter than milk, but the sins of the sons of Adam turned it black' (Jami` at-Tirmidhi). At the age of 35, the Prophet ﷺ peacefully resolved a fierce tribal feud by having all clan leaders lift the stone on a cloak, personally placing it with his noble hands.
Visiting Hours & Best Timings
Viewable 24 hours. Kissing or touching is subject to extreme crowd density and dedicated guard guidance. The green ceiling beacon denotes its alignment.
Mode of Transport & Access
Eastern cornerstone of the Kaaba on the central Mataf floor.
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Do not harm, shove, or elbow fellow Muslims to touch the stone. Kissing it is a Sunnah, but harming another Muslim is strictly Haram! The Prophet ﷺ taught that simply raising the right hand toward it from afar and saying 'Bismillahi Allahu Akbar' (Istilam) fulfills the complete Sunnah without sin.

Prophetic Footprint Maqam Ibrahim (Station of Abraham) مقام إبراهيم عليه السلام
Maqam Ibrahim 'Alayhis-Salam
Core Haram
Historical Background & Significance
A miraculous square stone upon which Prophet Ibrahim AS stood while raising the upper stone walls of the Kaaba. As the walls grew taller, the stone miraculously elevated under his feet, and his footprints were softened into the rock surface. Allah decreed in the Qur'an: 'And take the Station of Abraham as a place of prayer' (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:125). It is encased in an ornate crystal and gold hexagonal protective pavilion.
Visiting Hours & Best Timings
Viewable 24 hours during Tawaf. The footprints inside the gilded casing can be glimpsed through the thick crystal glass.

Mathaf Dar al-Madinah
Heritage Museums
Historical Background & Significance
The premier private museum dedicated specifically to the urban, architectural, and civil heritage of Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah. It features breathtaking large-scale architectural scale models depicting the Prophet's Mosque across its various historical expansions (from the prophetic era, the four Caliphs, Umayyad, Abbasid, and Ottoman eras to the modern Saudi expansions), meticulous topological models of prophetic battles, and hundreds of authentic relics and manuscripts.
Visiting Hours & Best Timings
Open Saturday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM (Closed Fridays). Nominal entry ticket fee.
Mode of Transport & Access
Located in the Knowledge Economic City (KEC), approximately 9 kilometers east of Al-Masjid an-Nabawi near the Haramain High-Speed Train Station (15 minutes by taxi).
Practical Tips & Etiquette
Hire one of the expert multilingual docents who provide fascinating deep dives into the scale models of the Prophet's house, the battlefields of Uhud and Khandaq, and ancient city gates. Highly recommended for pilgrims seeking historical visualization.

Rawdah ash-Sharifah Permit Booking (Nusuk App)

  • Download & Register: Install the official Saudi "Nusuk" app on iOS/Android. Create your account using your Saudi Visa Number and Passport Number before arrival.
  • Slot Release Timing: New weekly appointment slots are typically released on Fridays between 12:00 PM and 1:30 PM KSA time. Check frequently as cancellations pop up dynamically round the clock.
  • Men's Timings: Usually scheduled in two windows: late night after Isha (approx. 11:30 PM – 2:00 AM) and morning after Fajr (approx. 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM). Entry via Gate 38 (Bab Ali) or Gate 1 (Bab as-Salam).
  • Women's Timings: Usually scheduled in morning (approx. 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM) and night after Isha (approx. 9:30 PM – 12:00 AM). Entry strictly through Gate 37 (Bab Uthman) and Northern Plazas.
  • Permit Frequency: Saudi regulations permit 1 Rawdah visit every 365 days per pilgrim account. Ensure your QR code is saved offline as a screenshot on your phone in case data drops.
